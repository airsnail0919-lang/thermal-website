ThermalPro's thermal management solutions serve a broad spectrum of industries. Our engineering team has deep domain knowledge in each application area, enabling us to deliver optimized thermal designs that meet industry-specific requirements.

## LED Lighting

High-power LED lighting generates significant heat that must be efficiently managed to maintain lumen output, color consistency, and long service life.

**Our Solutions:**
- Aluminum extruded heatsinks for street lighting, high-bay, and floodlight fixtures
- Bonded fin heatsinks for compact COB LED modules with limited airflow
- Thermal interface materials (thermal pads, grease) for optimal LED-to-heatsink contact
- Passive and active cooling assemblies for high-lumen horticultural and stadium lighting

---

## Automotive & EV

The automotive industry presents unique thermal challenges — underhood temperatures, vibration, space constraints, and stringent reliability requirements. As vehicles electrify, thermal management becomes even more critical for battery packs, power electronics, and charging systems.

**Our Solutions:**
- Liquid cold plates for EV battery modules and battery packs
- Heat pipe assemblies for ADAS computing units and autonomous driving ECUs
- Custom heatsinks for on-board chargers (OBC), DC-DC converters, and inverters
- Thermal management for IGBT modules in traction inverters
- Compliant TIMs for automotive electronics with AEC-Q reliability requirements

---

## Telecom & 5G

5G base stations and RRUs (Remote Radio Units) pack high-power RF amplifiers into compact outdoor enclosures with passive cooling requirements. In telecom, thermal performance directly affects signal quality and network reliability.

**Our Solutions:**
- Large aluminum extrusion heatsinks with optimized fin pitch for natural convection
- Skived fin heatsinks for high-density BBU (Base Band Unit) applications
- Heat pipe embedded baseplates for RRU heat spreading
- Fan tray assemblies for indoor telecom cabinets
- Corrosion-resistant surface treatments for outdoor and coastal environments

---

## Medical Devices

Medical equipment demands precision thermal management with strict safety and regulatory compliance. From diagnostic imaging to laser surgery systems, reliable cooling is essential for both performance and patient safety.

**Our Solutions:**
- Custom cold plates for MRI gradient coils and CT scanner X-ray tubes
- Liquid cooling systems for medical laser diodes and surgical instruments
- Quiet fan modules with low vibration for patient-near equipment
- Thermally conductive yet electrically insulating materials for sensitive electronics
- Biocompatible surface finishes where required

---

## Power Electronics

Industrial power electronics — IGBT modules, SiC MOSFETs, rectifiers, and inverters — generate substantial heat that must be managed to maintain efficiency and prevent failure.

**Our Solutions:**
- High-performance bonded fin and skived fin heatsinks for IGBT modules
- Embedded tube cold plates for high-power inverters and converters
- Phase change thermal interface materials for power modules
- Forced air cooling assemblies with customized fan trays
- Liquid cooling solutions for high-power density applications (1kW+)

---

## Renewable Energy & Energy Storage

Solar inverters, wind turbine converters, and battery energy storage systems (BESS) require robust thermal management for outdoor, year-round operation across diverse climates.

**Our Solutions:**
- Corrosion-resistant heatsinks for solar microinverters and string inverters
- Liquid cooling plates for utility-scale BESS cabinets
- Fan assemblies for converter cabinets with IP65 protection
- Heat pipe cooling for power conditioning systems
- Passive cooling designs for zero-maintenance field operation

---

## Data Center & Cloud Computing

High-performance computing and AI data centers push thermal densities to new limits. Effective cooling directly impacts compute performance, energy efficiency, and operational costs.

**Our Solutions:**
- Liquid cold plates for CPU/GPU direct-to-chip cooling
- Rack-level CDU (Coolant Distribution Unit) integration support
- High-density fin heatsinks for server modules
- Custom cold plate and heat pipe assemblies for networking switches
- Thermal simulation services for data center airflow optimization
