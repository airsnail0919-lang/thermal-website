ThermalPro is a leading manufacturer of thermal management solutions, specializing in custom heat sinks, heat pipes, vapor chambers, cold plates, and thermal interface materials. With over a decade of engineering experience, we serve clients worldwide across LED lighting, automotive, telecom, medical, and industrial power electronics sectors.

## Core Strengths

### Engineering Excellence
Our in-house R&D team specializes in thermal simulation, mechanical design, and material science. We use industry-leading CFD software to optimize every design before a single prototype is made.

### Advanced Manufacturing
We operate a vertically integrated factory with CNC machining centers, extrusion presses, stamping lines, skiving equipment, and automated assembly lines. This vertical integration gives us full control over quality, cost, and lead time.

### Quality First
Every product goes through rigorous inspection. Our quality system includes incoming material inspection (IQC), in-process quality control (IPQC), and final quality assurance (OQC). We maintain full traceability from raw material to finished goods.

### Global Reach
With export experience to over 30 countries, we understand international quality standards, documentation requirements, and logistics. Our team is experienced in working with global OEMs, distributors, and engineering firms.

## Our Factory

### Manufacturing Equipment
- **Extrusion Presses**: 600T – 3600T for aluminum profiles up to 450mm wide
- **CNC Machining Centers**: 3-axis, 4-axis, and 5-axis for precision machining
- **Skiving Machines**: Full automated skiving lines for high-density fin heatsinks
- **Stamping Presses**: 25T – 200T for stamped fin and clip manufacturing
- **Heat Pipe Production Line**: Sintering, welding, charging, and aging stations
- **Vacuum Brazing Furnace**: For cold plate and vapor chamber assembly
- **Welding Robots**: Automated TIG and laser welding for liquid cooling assemblies

### Testing & Laboratory
- **Thermal Resistance Testers**: ASTM D5470 compliant
- **Wind Tunnel**: For airflow and pressure drop characterization
- **Thermal Imaging Cameras**: FLIR high-resolution IR cameras
- **Thermal Cycling Chambers**: -40°C to +150°C
- **Salt Spray Chamber**: Corrosion testing per ASTM B117
- **Helium Leak Detector**: For liquid cooling product integrity testing
