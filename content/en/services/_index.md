ThermalPro provides comprehensive thermal engineering services to help you bring your product to market faster. Our team of experienced thermal engineers works closely with your design team to develop optimized thermal solutions.

<!--more-->

## Our Service Process

### 1. Requirements Consultation
We begin by understanding your thermal requirements: power dissipation, ambient conditions, space constraints, target temperatures, and budget. Share your mechanical drawings, PCB layouts, or CAD models, and we'll provide an initial feasibility assessment.

### 2. Concept Design & Thermal Simulation
Our engineering team creates conceptual designs using advanced CFD thermal simulation software (Ansys Icepak, Flotherm, COMSOL). We optimize fin geometry, material selection, airflow paths, and interface materials to achieve your thermal targets.

### 3. Prototyping & Validation
Rapid prototyping with in-house CNC machining, 3D printing, and sample fabrication. We perform thermal performance testing in our lab to validate simulation results. Iteration cycles typically take 3–7 working days.

### 4. Mass Production
Once the design is validated, we move to production tooling and volume manufacturing. Our factory operates ISO9001-certified production lines with SPC quality control. Lead times range from 15–30 days depending on complexity and volume.
