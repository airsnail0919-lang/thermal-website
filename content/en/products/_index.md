## Heat Sinks

High-efficiency heat sinks in multiple construction types to suit your design and thermal budget.

| Type | Features | Applications |
|------|----------|--------------|
| **Aluminum Extrusion** | Cost-effective, complex geometries, 6063-T5 aluminum | LED lighting, power supplies, general electronics |
| **Skived Fin** | Ultra-dense fin pitch, excellent surface finish | High-power IGBTs, telecom base stations |
| **Bonded Fin** | High aspect ratio fins, mixed materials (Al + Cu) | High-density power electronics, industrial drives |
| **Stamped Fin** | Lightweight, high-volume production | Consumer electronics, automotive modules |

**Key Features:**
- Custom fin density, height, and base thickness
- Surface treatments: anodizing, nickel plating, powder coating
- Thermal simulation and design optimization available
- Prototype turnaround: 5–10 working days
- Production capacity: up to 500,000 units/month

| Type | Diameter / Thickness | Max Power |

| **Ultra-Thin Vapor Chamber** | 0.4mm – 3.0mm | 50–250W |

| Type | Cooling Capacity | Best For |
|------|------------------|----------|
| **Tube Cold Plate** | 500–1500W | IGBT modules, laser diodes |
| **Embedded Tube Cold Plate** | 800–3000W | High-power inverters, EV chargers |

**Key Features:**

- Multiple coolant channel designs (serpentine, parallel, pin-fin)
- Leak tested at 10 bar (145 psi)
- Custom inlet/outlet fittings (G1/4, NPT, quick disconnect)
- Fittings for water, glycol-water, or dielectric fluids

## Thermal Interface Materials

High-performance TIMs for minimizing contact resistance between components and heat sinks.

| Type | Thermal Conductivity | Key Benefit |
|------|----------------------|-------------|
| **Thermal Pad** | 1.0 – 15.0 W/mK | Easy installation, reworkable, no curing |
| **Thermal Grease** | 2.0 – 12.0 W/mK | Ultra-thin bond line, lowest thermal resistance |
| **Phase Change Material** | 3.0 – 8.0 W/mK | Liquid at operating temperature for gap filling |
| **Thermal Adhesive Tape** | 0.6 – 3.0 W/mK | Bonding + thermal management in one layer |

**Key Features:**
- Custom die-cut shapes and sizes
- Electrical insulation or conductive options
- UL 94 V-0 flame retardant grades available
- Shelf-stable, no refrigeration required
- RoHS and REACH compliant
